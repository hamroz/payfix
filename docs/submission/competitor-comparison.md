# Competitor comparison (draft for team review)

Researched Oct 5, 2026 with web search. Every claim about another product links to its source. "Unknown" means the sources we could reach don't say; it does **not** mean the product lacks the feature.

Some vendor help pages (help.coinbase.com, support.bitpay.com, help.request.finance, request.finance) blocked automated fetching. Claims sourced only from those pages' search-result excerpts are marked †, and a person should open them and confirm before we publish.

**We don't claim PayFix is unique.** Several products below handle parts of this problem, and some do so at far larger scale. Our proposed difference is narrower: the customer and the business agree on one versioned, hash-approved plan for the excess (split across invoices, credit, and refund), followed by a merchant-signed refund and a shared receipt. We haven't validated with customers that this matters to them; see `docs/evidence-log.md`.

## Honest read

- **BitPay** is the closest incumbent on exceptions. It supports Solana, sends invoices, detects over- and underpayments, and has a customer-initiated refund flow at scale. Based on its own support text, PayFix's differences are letting the customer **apply excess to another invoice or credit**, and binding the approval to an exact plan. Verify † before using this.
- **BTCPay Server** already has a customer-claims, merchant-approves refund pattern, but appears to focus on Bitcoin.
- **Request Finance** is stronger on invoicing breadth and accounting integrations. We found no evidence either way on exception resolution.
- **MoonPay Commerce** covers checkout and Shopify refunds on Solana, but its FAQ leaves refunds to the merchant and the customer to arrange.
- PayFix is a hackathon beta on devnet with no real users yet. Any comparison slide must say that.
